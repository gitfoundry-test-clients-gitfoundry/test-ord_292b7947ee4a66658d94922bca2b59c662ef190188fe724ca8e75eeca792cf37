Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you get to a broken boiler?

We aim to reach Stoke-on-Trent homes as soon as possible, often same day for breakdowns. Send an enquiry and we'll tell you straight when we can come.

Do you work with landlords?

Yes. We handle servicing and repairs for rental properties across North Staffordshire, and can work around tenant availability.

Should I repair or replace my boiler?

We'll tell you honestly. If a repair is economical, we'll do it. If the boiler's on its last legs, we'll say so and quote for a replacement.

How long does a bathroom installation take?

It depends on the size of the job, but we'll give you a clear timescale before starting and keep disruption to a minimum.

Do you give free quotes?

Yes. Send an enquiry through the form with a few details about the job and we'll come back with a quote, no obligation.

What areas do you cover?

Stoke-on-Trent and the surrounding North Staffordshire area. If you're just outside, ask anyway and we'll confirm.
